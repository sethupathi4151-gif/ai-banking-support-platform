REFUND AND TRANSACTION DISPUTE POLICY

Refund Processing

Refunds are not automatically guaranteed when a customer reports a transaction issue.

The transaction must be reviewed before a refund is confirmed.

Duplicate Transactions

If a customer reports being charged twice for the same purchase, the transaction should be investigated to determine whether a genuine duplicate transaction occurred.

A refund should not be confirmed before the duplicate transaction has been verified.

Unauthorized Transactions

Unauthorized transactions should be reported immediately.

Unauthorized transaction cases require investigation and may require customer verification.

High-risk unauthorized transaction cases should be escalated for human review.

Merchant Refunds

Some refunds depend on the merchant's refund process.

The support team should not provide a specific refund completion date unless the applicable policy or transaction information confirms it.

Refund Communication

Customer support responses should clearly explain that the transaction must be reviewed before a refund can be confirmed.

Agents must not claim that a refund has been processed unless the system confirms that the refund was completed.
