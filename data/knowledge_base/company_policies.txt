BANKING COMPANY POLICIES

Customer Verification Policy

Customer verification is required before accessing sensitive account information or performing sensitive banking operations.

Customers may be asked to verify their identity before account-specific information is provided.

Account Information Policy

Customers can request general information about account types, account status, and available banking services.

Sensitive account information should only be provided after successful customer verification.

Transaction Dispute Policy

Customers can report incorrect, duplicate, or unauthorized transactions.

Transaction disputes must be reviewed before any refund or adjustment is confirmed.

Duplicate transactions should be investigated to verify that the same transaction was processed more than once.

Fraud Policy

Unauthorized transactions and suspected fraudulent activity must be treated as high-priority cases.

Fraud-related cases may require additional customer verification and human review.

High-risk fraud cases must be escalated to the appropriate support team.

Card Security Policy

Lost or stolen cards should be reported immediately.

Card blocking and replacement requests may require customer verification.

Loan Policy

Loan-related requests may require verification of customer account information.

Customers can request information about loan eligibility, repayment schedules, interest rates, and outstanding balances.

Escalation Policy

High-risk fraud, unauthorized transactions, suspicious activity, and unresolved financial disputes should be escalated for human review.

Customer support agents should not confirm sensitive financial actions without appropriate verification.
