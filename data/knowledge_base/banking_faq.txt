BANKING FAQ

Account Information
Customers can view their account balance, account status, and account type through the banking support system.

Transaction Dispute
Customers can raise a dispute for an incorrect, duplicate, or unauthorized transaction.
Duplicate transactions should be reviewed before a refund is processed.

Fraud and Unauthorized Transactions
Customers should immediately report unauthorized transactions.
Fraud-related cases should be treated as high-priority cases and may require additional verification.

Card Support
Customers can report card blocking, card activation, lost cards, stolen cards, and card transaction issues.

Loan Support
Customers can request information about loan eligibility, repayment schedules, interest rates, and outstanding balances.
Loan-related requests may require verification of the customer's account information.

Payment Issues
Payment failures can occur because of insufficient balance, bank-side processing issues, network problems, or merchant-side issues.
Customers should verify the transaction status before attempting the payment again.

Refunds
Refund processing time depends on the transaction type and merchant.
A duplicate or unauthorized transaction should be investigated before confirming a refund.

Customer Verification
Sensitive banking operations require customer verification before any action is performed.

Escalation
High-risk fraud, unauthorized transactions, suspicious activity, and unresolved financial disputes should be escalated for human review.
